## Design Context

### Users
Families use Memory Project to capture a parent or grandparent's stories before they are lost. The core job is turning scattered memories, photos, and audio into a warm, printable keepsake book that feels worth saving and sharing.

### Brand Personality
Warm, inviting, and quietly premium. The interface should feel trustworthy, calm, and a little editorial rather than corporate or SaaS-heavy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-first UI with cream and paper tones, classic readable typography, and restrained bronze or green accents. Printed-book cues should feel tactile and sentimental, not ornate or nostalgic to the point of kitsch.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the UI emotionally warm without making it busy.
2. Treat books and memories like keepsakes, not generic records.
3. Make paid plan choices feel clear and per-book, not buried.
4. Preserve mobile usability while keeping the editorial desktop tone.
5. Prefer soft contrast, honest copy, and simple flows over flashy growth UI.
